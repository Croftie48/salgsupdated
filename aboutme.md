<main class="container prose about-page">
  <div class="callout">
    <b>A community built around growing.</b> More than 200 full and half plots sit within the heart of Seaford, bringing together growers, gardeners, volunteers and wildlife.
  </div>

  <img src="{{ '/assets/img/Carrot.JPG' | relative_url }}" alt="Seaford Allotments">

  <div class="colour-panel green-panel">
    <h2>Our allotments</h2>
    <p>Seaford Allotments are situated within the residential area of Seaford enclosed by Vale Road, Hindover Road, Haven Brow and Sutton Drove, with the main entrance in Sutton Drove. They are available for Seaford and Bishopstone residents. There are over 200 allotments consisting of full and half plots.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="colour-panel sand-panel">
    <h2>More than a place to grow</h2>
    <p>There is a mains water supply feeding water tanks positioned around the allotment site. Also on site is a large shed where compost, seeds and second-hand tools can be purchased by plot holders and members of Seaford Horticultural Society.</p>
    <p>In 2015, with the help of grants from the National Lottery and Seaford Town Council, a compost toilet was purchased. Solar power is used for the fan and lighting, and also provides winter lighting for the sales shed.</p>
  </div>

  <h2>Wildlife and open space</h2>
  <img src="{{ '/assets/images/banner1 (1).jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Allotment site">

  <div class="colour-panel lime-panel">
    <p>Half of the allotment site is flat and the remaining area is on a slope. On part of the land on the Vale Road boundary, a line of native British trees were planted as whips in 2015 which now provides excellent autumnal colour and a home for birds and other wildlife.</p>
    <p>This protects a conservation area of a wildflower meadow and micro orchard. There is also a Hazel Copse surrounded by a dead hedge, also providing a home for wildlife. This was all achievable with the help of the grants and our volunteers.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="callout">
    <b>Open Day</b><br>
    The Allotment Site has an open day annually in June in aid of the National Garden Scheme charities.
  </div>

  <h2>History</h2>

  <div class="timeline-block">
    <div class="timeline-item">
      <span>1922</span>
      <p>The allotment site was purchased by the Urban District Council of Seaford for use as allotments for the ‘labouring’ population of Seaford.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="timeline-item">
      <span>1986</span>
      <p>A group of plot holders formed a Society to keep the site organised and tidy, give plot holders a voice, and help prevent the land being taken over for development by the landowners, Lewes District Council. The Society was named as the Allotment Section of Seaford &amp; District Horticultural Society.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="timeline-item">
      <span>1990</span>
      <p>The Seaford Allotment Society was formed as an independent group.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="timeline-item">
      <span>1999–2000</span>
      <p>In 1999 the site was transferred from Lewes District Council to Seaford Town Council. In March 2000 the Society negotiated a self-management agreement with Seaford Town Council, who are the landlords.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
